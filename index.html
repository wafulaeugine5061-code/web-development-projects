Smart Health Management System

The Smart Health Management System is a modern digital health platform designed to streamline patient medical records, simplify online appointment scheduling, and enhance healthcare service delivery. It provides a secure, centralized portal where patients, medical practitioners, and administrators can track treatments and view health records seamlessly in real time.

Key System Features

  • Patient digital medical history and electronic health records (EHR)
  • Real-time online doctor appointment scheduling and booking
  • Secure patient portal for accessing lab results and prescription details
  • Automated SMS and email emergency reminders for upcoming hospital visits
  • Role-based access control for doctors, patients, and healthcare administrators
